How I work

Frame before I draw

The biggest wins in my work came from changing the question, not the UI. I spend real time with data, support tickets and customers before opening Figma.

Design with engineering

I prototype in code when it matters, pair with engineers on edge cases, and treat technical constraints as design material.

Raise the bar around me

Critique rituals, hiring loops, mentoring and systems work — I measure my impact by how good the team's work gets, not just mine.
