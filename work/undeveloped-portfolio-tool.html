Portfolio management tool

Undeveloped, now Dan.com, is a marketplace for buying and selling domain names. Sales grew almost in step with the size of the inventory, but there was no automated way for sellers to add domains. I redesigned the portfolio tool so sellers could manage large numbers of them.

Discipline
UX/UI design
Company
Undeveloped
Year
2016
Platform
Web

01
Problem

The company had focused on buyers. To grow, it needed more domains on the platform, and that meant caring about sellers.

02
What I did

I started with support: the most common requests, and contacts with the most active sellers. With my PM I interviewed them, and I read dozens of threads on domain forums to check that the loudest sellers spoke for everyone.

The biggest insight: big sellers managed all their domains and prices in Excel, which we hadn’t expected. And most sellers try a new platform with a few domains first, so the tool had to work for bulk uploads and small ones. Sketches became wireframes and prototypes, refined after rounds of remote usability testing.

During development we found that processing a big file could take 5 to 30 minutes. So I designed the wait: a friendly message about what happens next, then an email and an in-app message with a link to the results.

03
Impact

After launch, the platform’s inventory doubled. Engineers and success managers stopped spending their time uploading domains by hand.

04
What I learned

We spent 2.5 months polishing every pixel instead of shipping in steps and getting feedback sooner. And always challenge “known facts”: our customer journeys didn’t match reality, and we neglected the voice of our users for too long.
