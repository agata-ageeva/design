About me

Hi, I'm Agata

I’m a senior product designer with over ten years of experience. I specialise in reshaping how a product works, especially on two-sided platforms where design decisions affect revenue, and I judge my work by what it changes for users and the business.

I think in systems and care about the details. I work with product and engineering to decide what’s worth building, then stay close to the build through design systems and prototypes, so what ships matches the intent.

I’m based in Amsterdam.
