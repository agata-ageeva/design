Your portrait here

I'm a senior product designer with ten years of experience making complex products feel simple — in fintech, healthcare and design systems.

I started out as a visual designer at an agency, moved into product through a love of research, and have spent the last six years in-house on products where getting the details wrong costs people money or health. That's made me rigorous about evidence and a little obsessive about edge cases.

Today I'm at my best on ambiguous, cross-team problems: framing what we should build with product and engineering leads, shipping it with a small squad, and building the systems and rituals that help other designers do their best work.

Outside work, I mentor early-career designers through ADPList, write occasionally about design systems, and spend too much time at the pottery wheel.

What I believe about design

  1. The brief is a hypothesis

    My job is to find the problem worth solving, which isn't always the one I was handed.

  2. Clarity is kindness

    Especially when people are stressed, rushed or unsure. Plain words beat clever ones.

  3. Ship, then learn

    Good-enough in customers' hands teaches more than perfect in Figma. I plan for measurement from day one.

  4. Accessibility is the baseline

    Not a phase, not a checklist at the end. Designing for the edges improves the middle.
