Bio

I'm a senior product designer with 10+ years of experience working in startups, directly in product. I make complex products feel simple, mostly in fintech, healthcare and design systems.

I started as a visual designer at an agency and moved into product through a love of research. For the last six years I've worked in-house on products where getting details wrong costs people money or health. That made me rigorous about evidence and a little obsessive about edge cases.

I do my best work on ambiguous, cross-team problems: framing what to build with product and engineering leads, shipping it with a small squad, and building the systems that help other designers do their best work.

Outside work, I mentor early-career designers and spend too much time at the pottery wheel.

03
How I work

The brief is a hypothesis

  1. Frame before I draw

    My biggest wins came from changing the question, not the UI. I spend real time with data, support tickets and customers before opening Figma.

  2. Design with engineering

    I prototype in code when it matters and treat technical constraints as design material.

  3. Accessibility is the baseline

    Not a phase or a checklist at the end. Designing for the edges improves the middle.

  4. Raise the bar around me

    Critique rituals, hiring, mentoring and systems work. I measure my impact by how good the team's work gets.
